How it works

Send a link. Get a document.

No call, no quote, no price list. The free report is the same document a paid one is; what you pay for later is repetition and depth on a target team.

  1. You send the player's Transfermarkt linkIt is the one link that identifies a player beyond doubt. A name alone gets matched to the wrong person more often than you would think.
  2. We check coverageThe player needs at least one season of 270 or more minutes in a competition we hold. If he is outside our data we reply within 48 hours saying exactly that.
  3. The report is built and read by a personThe engine drafts it; a person checks every number against the source before it goes out. Sections without enough data print a stated limit, not a guess.
  4. You get a PDF within 48 hoursYours to forward. If it changes a decision, tell us which one; that is the only thing we ask for.
