The thesis

Underscouted is not the same as untalented.

The big platforms index a thousand competitions and genuinely watch a few dozen. Everywhere else, players are priced on highlight reels and word of mouth, and sellers outnumber analysts. That mispricing is structural, and it is where we work.

So every number here carries its own limits. Per-90s computed only over the minutes in which a stat was actually collected. Percentiles only where ten players clear the floor. A rating published next to its cohort size and its backtest. Nothing on this site is a number we could not reproduce for you on request.
