The method is published. So is the backtest.

Everyone in this market hides their model. Ours is replayed in public against real transfers, weak results included.

2,053 transfers replayed, scored on the rating held before the move
+0.268 rating vs fee paid, Spearman on 905 disclosed fees
41% / 27% top vs bottom quartile holding 1,350+ minutes at the new club (n=511/515)
+0.116 the weak result, published: rating vs minutes retention, all 2053 moves. Not a prediction of who sticks

Method level-v2.3, run 2 Sep 2026. A hit: dest league minutes >= 1350 in best of first two seasons. Identity is joined on name plus birth year; retention is availability-inclusive, so injuries count as failures. Full derivation and every stated limit on the methodology page.
