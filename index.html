Receipts

The method is published. So is the backtest.

Everyone in this market hides their model. Ours is written down and replayed against transfers that actually happened — and reported with the results that flatter it next to the ones that don't.

522 transfers replayed — moves abroad out of the covered competitions, scored on the rating each player held before he moved
+0.278 rating vs fee paid — Spearman correlation on the 223 permanent moves with a disclosed fee
36% / 29% top vs bottom quartile — share holding 1,350+ league minutes at the new club (n=131 / 132)
+0.069 the weak result, published — rating vs raw minutes retention across all 522 moves, which is why we call it a corridor-calibrated rating and not a prediction of who sticks

Method level-v1.1, run 15 Aug 2026, definition of a hit: dest league minutes >= 1350 in best of first two seasons. Identity is joined on normalised name plus birth year; minutes retention is availability-inclusive, so injuries count as failures. The full derivation, the coverage table and every stated limit are on the methodology page — including the limits that make us look worse.
