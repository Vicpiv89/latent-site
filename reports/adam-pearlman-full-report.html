Stated limits & methodology

How to read this report

Every number in this report is computed from sources we name, under rules we publish, with its gaps stated. What follows is the full list of methods and limits - including the things this report deliberately does not claim.

  • Percentiles. Computed within one league, one season, one position group, mid-rank with ties split. Published only when at least 10 players clear the minutes gate in that peer group (270′ in our top-flight coverage, 450′ in second-tier feeder coverage) - below that the percentile is withheld, never guessed. The peer-group n is printed beside every percentile in this report.
  • Missing is never zero. A metric a competition does not collect renders as “not recorded”; a rate whose sample is below the minutes gate renders as “—”. Neither is ever shown as zero, and no number in this report is imputed.
  • Cross-league comparisons. Raw percentiles are never compared across leagues or tiers. The only cross-league number we publish is the Latent Level, which is corridor-calibrated and backtested against post-move outcomes before it appears in any report.
  • Export Index. A composite score, not a percentile: an average of five outfield percentile components with a U-21 multiplier, so it can exceed 100. Goalkeepers carry no Export Index - it contains no goalkeeping.
  • Transfer history. From our own Transfermarkt-sourced transfer record. Where identity is matched by name rather than a verified ID, the report says so and does not present the history as fact.

On step-up moves only (destination fitted stronger than origin): top-quartile 34% (n=279) vs bottom-quartile 20% (n=271). Identity join is by normalized name + birth-year (22 ambiguous names dropped); minutes retention is an availability-inclusive outcome - injuries count as failures. Not yet in our data

Contract terms. Contract-expiry dates exist at source but are not yet in our databases, so no figure in this report leans on contract length.
