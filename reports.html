Ongoing coverage

One report is a snapshot. A cadence is an edge.

  • Per match: rolling form, usage and opposition context, while the match is still the news.
  • Monthly: a player or a shortlist, and a straight answer on whether the case is strengthening.
  • Per season: a club portfolio kept current, ready before each window opens.

No public price list: scoped and quoted within 24 hours, delivered within 48. Every report closes with a stated-limits section; how every number is made is public.
